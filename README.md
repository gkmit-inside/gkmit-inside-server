# GKMIT INSIDE: Project Overview, Mission, and Tech Stack

## 1. Overview

**GKMIT INSIDE** is a modern, high-performance knowledge-sharing platform developed exclusively for the **GKMIT Organisation**.

It functions as a secure, internal hub dedicated to capturing, organizing, and distributing long-form content, technical discussions, key insights, and official announcements among team members.

The platform employs a hybrid architecture, utilizing the strength of the **full-stack JavaScript ecosystem** (a modified MERN stack) for a unified development experience, combined with specialized hosting for both the frontend and backend.

---

## 2. Mission

The core mission of **GKMIT INSIDE** is to transform internal communication into an organized knowledge library.  
Its driving philosophy is to be **“Twitter for GKMIT”**, but focused purely on structured, high-quality knowledge rather than casual social interaction.

### Core Pillars of the Mission

- **Knowledge Preservation:** Establish a persistent internal library of organizational expertise and institutional memory.
- **Structured Learning:** Facilitate continuous growth and professional development through easily discoverable, organized content.
- **Community Collaboration:** Create a dedicated space for Developers and Administrators to share technical solutions and insights efficiently.
- **Quality & Integrity:** Maintain content standards through a structured review and moderation process.
